Room Acoustics Simulator

Draw your room outline corner by corner (or start from the rectangle), pick a material for each wall, and place sound sources and a listener. Reverberation, room modes, and sound pressure levels update live as you drag. Flip between the 2-D plan and a 3-D view you can orbit. The URL tracks your setup, so you can bookmark or share it.

Room view

Drag the numbered sources, the listener (L), and the corner handles. Click a wall to jump to its material. Hover anywhere in the room for the level at that spot. Use “Draw room outline” to sketch any shape — L-shapes, angled walls, whatever your space is.

Assumptions: the room is a straight-walled prism (any polygon floor plan, constant height). Statistical formulas (Sabine, Eyring) assume a reasonably diffuse field and are most reliable above the Schroeder frequency; the mode list assumes rigid walls and a rectangular plan. Absorption coefficients, RT60, modal and Bolt-area calculations use ADA's own physics engine, so they match the Modal Analysis and RT60 Calculator tabs. The steady-state SPL model and heat map have no counterpart in those tabs — ADA models no sound field — but they are driven by the same absorption figures. See static/roomsim/js/engine.js.
